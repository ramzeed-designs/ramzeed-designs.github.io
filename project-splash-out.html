Story

A tiny fish escapes a restaurant kitchen and journeys across a lethal human world, back to the sea.

What makes it different

Realistic fish movement meets physics-based puzzles: water flow and clumsy controls create the fun and the challenge.

03Survival mechanics

Every second out of water counts.

Dehydration timer

A fish can't survive out of water for long. Every puddle buys you time.

find water… fast

Flop physics

Skill-based movement driven by weight and momentum, so every jump is a little bit clumsy.

What you land on changes everything

  • 💧

    Water

    Normal mobility.

  • 🛢️

    Oil

    High speed, low control.

  • 🔥

    Hot / chemical

    Rapid health drain.

  • 🧊

    Ice

    Uncontrolled sliding.

04My part

Where I lived: the code.

This was a group project. I contributed heavily to the gameplay and the coding, and it's the part of the project I loved most: turning the rules above into something that actually plays in Unity.

// the core loop, in plain words
void Update() {
    if (fish.InWater)  dryness.Refill();
    else               dryness.Drain(surface.rate);

    fish.Flop(input, surface.grip);   // oil, ice…

    if (dryness.Empty)  GameOver("dried out");
    if (fish.AtExit)    NextLevel();
}
  • Gameplay shaping how the fish moves, jumps and reacts to each surface.
  • Coding the interactions and puzzle logic that open up a path through the kitchen.
  • Playtesting and tuning, so the clumsiness feels fun rather than frustrating.
