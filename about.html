About me

Hey, I'm Ramzi.

I'm a Play Experience Designer pursuing a Master of Design in Toy & Game Design at NID Gandhinagar. My work spans toys, games and experiences.

I love to tinker, and curiosity drives everything I do. I'm fascinated by why people behave the way they do: what makes them hesitate, what sparks delight, and how a thoughtful interaction can turn into a moment of joy, connection or surprise.

At my core, I bring creativity, empathy and positivity to both people and projects. I love making things that spark curiosity, encourage exploration and bring people together. For me, design goes beyond solving problems; it's about creating moments that feel alive, human and meaningful.
